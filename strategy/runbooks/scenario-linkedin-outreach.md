# 🤝 Runbook: LinkedIn Creator Outreach & Partnership Engine

> **Mode**: NEXUS-Micro to NEXUS-Sprint | **Duration**: 6-8 weeks to first partnerships, then ongoing | **Agents**: 6-9
> **Channel**: LinkedIn desktop (manual sends — no automation tools) | **Niche**: Land wholesaling education & influence

---

## Scenario

You want to build genuine relationships with the top creators, educators, and influencers in the **land wholesaling** space and convert a subset of those relationships into partnerships (co-marketing, affiliate/rev-share, joint content, referral pipelines, or product collaborations).

You are running this **manually from your desktop** — sending connection requests and DMs by hand, engaging authentically, and nurturing over time. This runbook gives you the target list logic, the profile setup, the exact multi-touch message sequences, the daily/weekly cadence that keeps you under LinkedIn's radar, and the partnership-conversion play.

**Core principle:** This is relationship-building, not spray-and-pray outbound. Every touch should give value before it asks for anything. The "pitch" is the *last* thing that happens, not the first.

## Goal & End State

| Horizon | Outcome |
|---------|---------|
| Week 2 | Profile optimized, target list of 50-100 creators built and tiered, warm-up engagement running |
| Week 4 | 40+ accepted connections, 15+ active conversations, 3-5 "warm" relationships |
| Week 8 | 2-4 partnership conversations opened, 1-2 pilot collaborations agreed (guest content, affiliate, or co-promo) |
| Ongoing | Repeatable weekly engine; reputation as a known, helpful name in the land space |

## Execution Plan

### Week 1: Foundation — Profile, Positioning, Target List

```
Day 1-2: Profile & Positioning  (Marketing LinkedIn Content Creator)
├── Rewrite headline → who you help + what you do in land, not "Founder | Entrepreneur"
│   e.g. "Helping land wholesalers & educators scale reach + revenue | Partnerships"
├── Rewrite About → your angle, credibility, and why creators should know you
├── Featured section → 1-2 proof assets (case study, results, a useful resource)
├── Banner + profile photo → clean, on-brand, approachable
└── Turn on Creator Mode; set 3-5 topics (#landwholesaling #realestateinvesting etc.)

Day 3-5: Target List Build  (Sales Account Strategist)
├── Source creators from:
│   ├── LinkedIn search: "land wholesaling", "land investing", "land flipping"
│   ├── People who post/comment on top land educators' content
│   ├── YouTube/podcast/IG land names → find their LinkedIn
│   └── "People also viewed" + 2nd-degree connections of top names
├── Tier the list:
│   ├── Tier 1 (10-15): Top educators/influencers — the dream partners
│   ├── Tier 2 (25-40): Mid-size creators, active posters, course sellers
│   └── Tier 3 (30-50): Rising voices, engaged community members (easiest yes)
└── For each Tier 1/2: note their offer, audience size, recent content, a genuine hook
```

**Build the list in a simple tracker** (Sheet or Notion). Columns:
`Name | Tier | Profile URL | Audience/Offer | Recent hook | Connection sent | Accepted | Last touch | Stage | Notes`

Stages: `Prospect → Connected → Engaged → Conversation → Partnership Talk → Partner`

### Week 2: Warm-Up — Get on Their Radar Before You DM

```
Warm-up engagement  (run daily, ~20-30 min)
├── Follow all Tier 1 & 2 creators (follow ≠ connect; no limit pressure)
├── Thoughtfully comment on 5-8 posts/day from your targets
│   → Add a real insight or ask a smart question. Never "Great post! 🔥"
├── Like + share 1-2 pieces with your own take added
└── Post your own content 2-3x/week (see cadence below) so your profile looks alive
```

> **Why warm up first:** When your connection request arrives, they've already seen your name in their comments 2-3 times. Accept rates jump dramatically. This single step is the difference between 20% and 60%+ acceptance.

### Week 3-4: Connect & Open — The Sequence

Send connection requests **only after** 2-3 warm-up touches on that person. Personalize every Tier 1/2 request.

**Cadence to stay safe (manual, from desktop):**
- **Connection requests:** 15-20/day max (LinkedIn's soft weekly cap is ~100-200; stay well under)
- **DMs / follow-ups:** 20-30/day max
- Spread across the day, not in one burst. Skip weekends for B2B.

#### Touch 1 — Connection Request (personalized, ≤300 chars)
> Hey [Name] — been following your stuff on [specific topic, e.g. "pulling data for land comps"] and your take on [recent post] actually changed how I think about [thing]. Would love to stay connected and follow your work.

*No pitch. No ask. Just a genuine, specific reason.*

#### Touch 2 — Value-First Note (1-2 days after they accept)
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! Genuinely enjoy your content on [topic]. Quick one — [share a relevant resource / insight / a result you got, OR ask a thoughtful question about their work]. No agenda, just liked what you're building.

*Give something. A stat, a tool, a compliment with substance, an intro. Zero ask.*

#### Touch 3 — Deepen (3-5 days later, only if they replied)
> Love that. [React to their reply.] I actually work with [land wholesalers / educators] on [your angle] — always trading notes with people doing interesting things in the space. What are you focused on growing right now?

*This surfaces their goals — the doorway to a partnership fit.*

#### Touch 4 — The Soft Pitch (only when there's clear mutual fit)
> Been thinking about what you said re: [their goal]. I've got [audience / distribution / product / skill] that lines up well with what you're doing. Might be worth a 15-min call to see if there's a way we could help each other's audiences — no pressure either way. Open to it?

> **Rule:** Never jump to Touch 4 early. If someone's cold, keep giving value (comment, share, tag) for weeks. The best partnerships come from the ones you *didn't* rush.

### Week 5-8: Convert — From Conversation to Partnership

```
Discovery call  (Sales Discovery Coach scripts this)
├── Understand their audience, offers, pain, and what they want more of
├── Find the overlap: your audience ↔ theirs, your skill ↔ their gap
└── Float 1-2 concrete collaboration shapes (below)

Structure the deal  (Sales Deal Strategist)
├── Guest content swap (you on their pod/newsletter, them on yours)
├── Affiliate / rev-share on each other's offers
├── Co-created resource or webinar to both audiences
├── Referral partnership (you send deals/leads, they send audience)
└── Sponsorship / paid collab if that's the model

Pilot small → prove value → expand
```

## Partnership Angles for the Land Wholesaling Space

Pick the ones that match what *you* actually bring:

- **Audience swap:** Cross-promote to each other's email/social lists.
- **Affiliate:** Earn on their course/tool sales; they earn on yours.
- **Co-content:** Joint webinar, YouTube collab, podcast guesting, co-authored guide.
- **Deal-flow / referral:** You feed vetted land deals or leads; they provide reach or capital.
- **White-label / service:** Offer your service (dispo, data, marketing, VA systems) to their students.
- **Community / mastermind:** Get placed as a trusted vendor/speaker inside their program.

## Weekly Content Cadence (credibility engine)

Outreach converts far better when your profile is actively posting. Aim for:

| Freq | Format | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| 2-3x/week | Text or carousel post on land insights, results, lessons | Show expertise, get seen |
| Daily | 5-8 substantive comments on targets' posts | Warm-up + visibility |
| 1x/week | Tag or celebrate a creator you admire (genuinely) | Relationship deposit |
| Ongoing | Reply to every comment on your posts | Signal you're active & human |

Have the **Marketing LinkedIn Content Creator** build a 4-week content calendar around land-space topics.

## Metrics — Track Weekly

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Connection requests sent | 75-100/week |
| Acceptance rate | 50%+ (higher with warm-up) |
| Replies to Touch 2 | 30%+ |
| Active conversations | 10-15 running |
| Partnership calls booked | 1-2/week by Week 5 |
| Pilot collaborations agreed | 1-2 by Week 8 |

If acceptance <35% → your requests aren't personalized enough or warm-up is too thin.
If replies <20% → your Touch 2 is asking, not giving.

## Guardrails & Compliance

- **Manual only.** No auto-connect / auto-DM bots — they get accounts restricted and read as spam. This runbook is built for hand-sent desktop outreach on purpose.
- **Respect LinkedIn limits:** ~100-200 connection requests/week is the danger zone; stay at 75-100. If you hit "You've reached the weekly invitation limit," stop and resume next week.
- **Personalize Tier 1/2 every time.** Generic requests tank your acceptance rate and your reputation.
- **Give 3x before you ask once.** The fastest way to burn a top creator is to pitch in the first message.
- **Track everything** so no one gets double-messaged or dropped mid-thread.
- **Play the long game.** The top 10-15 educators are worth 6 months of patient relationship-building, not one cold pitch.
